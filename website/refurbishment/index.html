A home refurbishment updates and reconfigures your existing property, whether that is a full renovation, a partial upgrade, or a single kitchen or bathroom. Eagle Build handles the structural work, the services and the high-end finishes as one coordinated project, so you deal with one accountable team rather than juggling separate trades.

We are a family-run, FMB-accredited builder based in Cheadle Hulme, working across Stockport, Greater Manchester and Cheshire. From sensitive period property renovations to knock-throughs, extensions folded into a wider refurbishment, and fast, well-planned bathroom and kitchen upgrades, we start with a realistic scope and budget and manage the work through to a clean hand-over.

Full house refurbishments

Whole-home renovations covering structural changes, upgraded plumbing and electrics, and high-end finishes, delivered in a sensible sequence that tackles structural fixes before cosmetic work.

Partial and room-by-room upgrades

Prefer to renovate in stages? We handle targeted refurbishments, a single floor, a kitchen, a bathroom, so you can improve your home at a pace that suits your budget.

Period property renovation

Older and character homes need a careful touch. We modernise services and comfort while respecting original features, so the finished result feels right for the property.

Kitchens and bathrooms

From complete kitchen refits to bathrooms transformed with meticulous planning and pre-ordered materials, we minimise disruption while delivering a quality, lasting finish.

Knock-throughs and reconfiguration

Removing walls and using structural steel to open up rooms, improve natural light and create the flowing, sociable spaces modern family life needs.

Extensions as part of a refurb

When more space is the goal, we fold an extension into the wider refurbishment so new and existing areas are designed, built and finished as one seamless project.

FAQs

Questions, answered

What does a home refurbishment involve?

A refurbishment can range from a single room to a whole house. Fuller projects typically include structural changes, upgraded systems like plumbing and electrics, and high-end finishes. We prioritise structural fixes before cosmetic work for a lasting result.

How much should I budget, and should I allow a contingency?

Costs vary widely with scope, so we start with a realistic budget for your project. We recommend setting aside a contingency of around 10 to 20 percent for surprises such as hidden structural issues uncovered once work begins.

Can you refurbish a period or older property?

Yes. We regularly renovate period and character homes across Cheshire and Stockport, upgrading services and comfort while respecting original features so the finished home stays true to the property.

How long does a refurbishment take?

It depends on scope. A well-planned bathroom can be transformed in around a week, while full-house refurbishments run longer. Careful planning and pre-ordered materials keep disruption to a minimum whatever the size.

Can an extension be part of the refurbishment?

Absolutely. We often combine extensions with wider refurbishments, and with our in-house design and build team the new and existing spaces are planned, built and finished as one coordinated project.
