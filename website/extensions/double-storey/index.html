A double storey extension is the ideal solution when you need to add significant space to your Cheadle or Stockport home. By extending upwards as well as out, you gain living space across two floors, from a larger kitchen-diner below to extra bedrooms or a bathroom above, while preserving your garden.

Eagle Build is a family-run, FMB-accredited builder serving Greater Manchester and Cheshire. Our in-house architects and engineers design bespoke double storey extensions tailored to your property, backed by excellent communication, regular progress updates and post-construction support.

FAQs

Questions, answered

Do I need planning permission for a double storey extension?

Double storey extensions usually require planning permission, as they typically fall outside permitted development limits. Our design-and-build team prepares and manages your application, along with building regulations, from start to finish.

How much does a double storey extension cost?

Cost varies with size, layout and specification, so figures differ significantly between projects. After your free consultation we provide a detailed, transparent quotation with no hidden costs.

How long does a double storey extension take?

Timelines depend on the scope of work, with more complex projects taking longer. We agree a realistic schedule with you upfront and keep you updated with regular progress reports.

Is a double storey extension better value than a single storey?

For maximising space, often yes. Building over two floors adds far more accommodation for a comparable footprint, making it cost-effective when you need significant extra room without moving.

Can a double storey extension match my existing house?

Yes. Our architects design each extension to complement your property, whether traditional or modern, matching brickwork, rooflines and proportions so the new space looks like it was always there.
