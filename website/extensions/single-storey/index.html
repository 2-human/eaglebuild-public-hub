A single storey extension is the most cost-effective way to add usable living space to your Cheadle or Stockport home and make it look and feel brand new. Because they involve less structural complexity than multi-storey builds, they are typically more affordable, faster to complete and often fall within permitted development.

Eagle Build is a family-run, FMB-accredited builder serving Greater Manchester and Cheshire. Our in-house architects design single storey extensions that blend seamlessly with your existing home, and a dedicated project manager keeps everything on track with honest pricing and no hidden costs.

FAQs

Questions, answered

Do I need planning permission for a single storey extension?

Many single storey extensions fall under permitted development rights, so no planning permission is needed, provided they stay within set size and height limits. We assess your project and handle any application required.

How much does a single storey extension cost?

Cost depends on size, specification and finish, so figures vary widely from project to project. We provide a detailed, no-obligation quotation with transparent pricing and no hidden costs after your free consultation.

How long does a single storey extension take to build?

Most single storey extensions take a few months from breaking ground to completion, depending on complexity. We agree a clear timeline with you before work starts and keep you updated throughout.

What can I use a single storey extension for?

They are highly versatile, ideal for open-plan kitchen-diners, an extra bedroom, a home office, a playroom or a larger living area. Our architects design the space around how you want to use it.

Will a single storey extension add value to my home?

Yes. A well-designed single storey extension adds usable space and lasting value, and is often far more cost-effective than moving to a larger property in the same area.
