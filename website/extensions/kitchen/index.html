FAQs

Questions, answered

How much value does a kitchen extension add?

A kitchen extension can add around 15% to your property's value, depending on location and finish. As the heart of the home, a bright open-plan kitchen is one of the most sought-after features for buyers.

Do I need planning permission for a kitchen extension?

Many single-storey rear and side kitchen extensions fall under permitted development, but limits on size and height apply. We assess your project and handle any planning application for you.

How can I get more natural light into my kitchen extension?

Bi-fold or sliding doors, roof lanterns, skylights and floor-to-ceiling glazing all draw daylight deep into open-plan spaces. Our architects design the glazing around how your kitchen faces the sun.

What is the difference between a kitchen extension and a refit?

A refit replaces units within your existing walls, while a kitchen extension adds floor space, letting you create an open-plan kitchen-diner, add a utility room and completely rethink the layout.

How long does a kitchen extension take?

Most single-storey kitchen extensions take a few months on site, depending on size and specification. We provide a clear timeline in your quotation before work begins.
