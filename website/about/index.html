A family-run builder that cares as much as you do

Eagle Build is a family-run, FMB-registered design-and-build company based in Cheadle Hulme. Led by David Eagle, we design and build high-end new homes, extensions and renovations across Stockport, Greater Manchester and Cheshire.

We founded Eagle Build on a simple idea: that building a home should be something you enjoy, not endure. By keeping our architects and construction team under one roof, we give every client one accountable point of contact, honest advice, and a finish we're proud to put our name to.
