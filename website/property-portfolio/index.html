Eagle Build helps aspiring and established landlords start, build and manage residential and commercial property portfolios across Cheadle Hulme, Stockport, Greater Manchester and Cheshire. Whether you are buying your first rental or scaling a portfolio, our support flexes to match, from light-touch acquisition advice early on to hands-on management as you grow.

As a family-run, FMB-accredited design-and-build company, we bring builder's eyes to every purchase and refurbishment. That means honest guidance on which properties to buy, how to prepare them for tenants, and how to protect your investment for the long term.

Acquisition Advice

We advise on which properties are worth buying and which to avoid, with building inspections to confirm structural integrity before you commit.

Refurbishment

We prepare properties for occupancy and invest in the interior to give each renovated property longevity and lasting rental appeal.

Ongoing Maintenance

Regular repairs and maintenance are a vital part of a healthy portfolio, and we keep your properties in reliable, lettable condition.

Tenant Marketing

We help secure the right tenants for your portfolio properties, reducing void periods and keeping your rental income steady.

Renewable Energy Upgrades

Adding solar panels to properties can reduce running costs and create additional revenue streams alongside your rental yield.

Scalable Support

Our involvement grows with your portfolio, from occasional consultation on a single property to end-to-end management across multiple sites.

What we include

FAQs

Questions, answered

Do I need an existing portfolio to work with you?

No. We help both aspiring and established landlords, from buying your first rental property to managing and scaling a larger portfolio across Greater Manchester and Cheshire.

What does your portfolio management cover?

Acquisition advice, refurbishment, ongoing maintenance and tenant marketing. Support scales from light-touch consultation early on to hands-on management as your portfolio grows.

Can you advise on which properties to buy?

Yes. We advise on which properties to purchase or avoid, and carry out building inspections to confirm structural integrity before you acquire them.

How can I add value to my rental properties?

We invest in quality interiors for longevity and rental appeal, and can add measures such as solar panels to reduce running costs and create additional revenue streams.

Do you offer meetings to discuss my portfolio?

Yes. We provide on-site meetings where our team reviews your properties and gives customised guidance tailored to your specific portfolio and goals.
