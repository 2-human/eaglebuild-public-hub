FAQs

Questions, answered

How much does it cost to build a new home in Cheshire or Greater Manchester?

Bespoke new build costs vary with size, site and specification, but most premium new homes in the area start from a mid-to-high four-figure sum per square metre. We provide a clear, itemised cost plan at design stage so your budget is realistic before you commit.

How long does it take to build a new home?

A bespoke new build typically takes 12 to 18 months from securing planning permission to handover, depending on size, ground conditions and specification. We manage the full programme so trades are sequenced and momentum holds.

Do you help find land or plots?

Yes. Alongside building on your own plot, we help source land and appraise development opportunities across Stockport, Cheadle, Bramhall and the wider Cheshire area, including knock-down rebuild sites.

Do you handle planning permission and architecture?

Yes. Our design-and-build service includes in-house architects who carry your project through the full RIBA workflow and manage the planning application with Stockport Council or the relevant Cheshire authority.

Are you accredited and insured?

Eagle Build is a family-run, Federation of Master Builders (FMB) member and an award-winning builder. Our new build projects are covered by an insurance-backed structural warranty.
