Eagle Build offers full architect services across Cheadle Hulme, Stockport and the wider Greater Manchester and Cheshire area. As a family-run, FMB-accredited design-and-build company, we keep our architects and construction team under one roof, so your design is shaped from day one by the people who will actually build it.

Led by David Eagle, we handle residential, commercial and interior design, guiding every project through a clear five-stage process from initial consultation to completion and handover, with no disconnect between the drawings and the build.

Residential Design

Custom homes, extensions and renovations tailored to how you live. Sustainable, energy-efficient designs that add space and long-term value to your property.

Commercial Design

Functional office, retail, hospitality and industrial spaces designed around your brand and how your business operates day to day.

Interior Design

Space planning and layout optimisation, material and finish selection, plus bespoke furniture and fixtures to complete the scheme.

Design-and-Build Under One Roof

Our in-house architects and builders work as one team, so decisions made on paper are grounded in what is genuinely buildable, on budget and on site.

A Documented Process

Every project follows five defined stages, giving you clarity on where things stand and what happens next at each point.

Lived-In Renovations

We plan work sensitively around families who need to stay in their home during the build, keeping disruption to a minimum.

What we include

Included as standard

  • Initial consultation, site assessment and budget and timeline discussion
  • Conceptual sketches, 3D models and visualisations
  • Design refinement based on your feedback
  • Comprehensive architectural drawings and planning documents
  • Permit and planning application assistance
  • Construction oversight with regular site visits and quality checks
  • Final inspection, documentation and post-completion support

From homes in Cheadle Hulme to commercial spaces across Stockport and Cheshire, our in-house architects design with local sites, styles and planning in mind.

FAQs

Questions, answered

What areas do you cover for architect services?

We are based in Cheadle Hulme and provide architect and design-and-build services across Stockport, Greater Manchester and Cheshire, covering both residential and commercial projects.

What is your design process?

Five stages: consultation, design development, project planning, construction oversight, then completion and handover. Each stage keeps you informed on progress and the next steps.

Do you handle both design and construction?

Yes. We are a design-and-build company with in-house architects, so the same team designs and builds your project, keeping the drawings and the finished result fully aligned.

Can I stay in my home during the work?

In many cases, yes. We plan renovations and extensions to work sensitively around families living on site, as our homeowner clients Pete and Claire experienced.

Do you help with planning permission?

Yes. Project planning includes comprehensive architectural drawings, stakeholder coordination and assistance with permits and planning applications.
