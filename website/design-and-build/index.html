Design and build means one team handles everything: our in-house designers, planners and construction crews take your project from initial concept through planning approval to a finished, handed-over home. Instead of hiring an architect and a builder separately and hoping they coordinate, you get a single point of accountability from start to finish.

Based in Cheadle Hulme and working across Stockport, Greater Manchester and Cheshire, Eagle Build is a family-run, FMB-accredited builder. We designed the process this way to solve the two problems every homeowner fears: delays and overspends. With one consistent team on your project, decisions are faster, drawings are buildable, and nothing gets lost between separate firms.

Single point of accountability

One team owns design, planning and construction, so there is no finger-pointing between architect and builder when questions arise. You always know exactly who is responsible.

In-house design team

Our designers work hand-in-hand with the crews who will actually build the project. That means drawings are realistic, buildable and costed properly from the outset.

Design that stays on budget

Because the people designing your home also price and build it, we catch cost issues on paper rather than on site. Fewer surprises, fewer expensive changes mid-build.

Planning handled for you

We prepare and manage your planning application and liaise with the local authority, guiding the project through the approvals stage so you are not left navigating it alone.

Structural know-how

We use structural steel and considered design to open up rooms, improve natural light and create spaces that maximise how you enjoy life at home.

One relationship throughout

A consistent team stays with you from concept to hand-over, reducing risk and stress so you can focus on the big picture rather than chasing multiple contractors.

FAQs

Questions, answered

What is design and build?

Design and build is a single-contract approach where one company handles design, planning and construction. Eagle Build's in-house team takes your project from concept through to finished build, giving you one point of accountability instead of separate architects and contractors.

How is this better than hiring an architect and builder separately?

With one team, design and construction stay joined up. Drawings are buildable and costed accurately, decisions are faster, and there is no gap where responsibility falls between two firms, which reduces delays, overspends and stress.

Do you handle planning permission?

Yes. We prepare and submit your planning application and liaise with the local authority on your behalf, guiding the project through the approvals stage as part of the design and build process.

What follows the RIBA plan of work stages?

Our process broadly mirrors the recognised design stages: brief and concept, developed design, planning, technical design and building regulations, construction, then hand-over, with the same team accountable across every stage.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Cheadle Hulme and work throughout Stockport, Greater Manchester and Cheshire. Get in touch via WhatsApp or our website form to discuss your design and build project.
